# ADR 0002: Season page swaps to recaps after the season ends

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The public season page at `/season/{id}` was built for an in-season audience: it leads with the current week's match results and a Previous/Next week navigator, followed by standings, upcoming schedule, and awards. After a season ends, visitors keep landing on this page (it's the canonical URL for a season), but the "current week" framing is no longer the right entry point — the last week's results are stale and there's no upcoming week.

The league wanted to author editorial content (champion announcements, season-end photos, longer narratives) and have it surface as the primary content on the season page once the season is over, without breaking deep links to specific weeks that visitors may have bookmarked or shared during the season.

## Decision
Introduce a "Season Highlights" block on `/season/{id}` that **replaces** the weekly results table and the week navigator when all three of the following are true:

1. The season has been over for more than 7 days — `today > (last week's date) + 7 days`.
2. At least one published `NewsPost` is tagged to the season via `WinterSeasonId`.
3. The URL has no explicit `?week=…` querystring.

When all three hold, the page renders a hero card for the most recently published recap and a 2-column card grid for the remaining recaps, in place of the weekly results and the Previous/Next nav. Standings, upcoming schedule, and awards continue to render below.

In recap mode, the Awards section also switches to whole-season scope (equivalent to `?allAwards=true`) since there is no "current week" being spotlighted.

When any of the three conditions fails — season still in-progress, recently ended but no recaps authored yet, or a deep link with `?week=…` — the page renders exactly as before.

## Consequences

- **Authoring is decoupled from the cutover.** Admins can publish recaps any time during or after the season. They only become the page's primary content once both the time threshold and the data-presence check are satisfied — there is no admin toggle to flip.
- **Deep links continue to work.** Any `/season/{id}?week=YYYY-MM-DD` URL — including those generated by the in-season Previous/Next nav and any shared or bookmarked links — bypasses recap mode and renders the requested week's results. Visitors who want to browse past weekly results from a concluded season can do so via `/season/{id}/schedule` or by clicking through to specific matches.
- **7-day grace window.** The threshold is `> 7 days past the last week`, not `≥ 0`. This keeps the weekly results visible for a week after the final week so visitors arriving immediately after the last night of play still see the scores. It also gives admins a natural week to compose recaps before the page changes its layout under them.
- **No recap, no cutover.** If a season ends and no recaps are ever authored, the page permanently shows the last week's results as the "current week" — same behaviour the page has always had for concluded seasons. The cutover is opt-in via authoring, not automatic.
- **Public heading is "Season Highlights", not "Season Recaps".** The data filter is `WinterSeasonId == season.Id` regardless of `PostType`, so a season-tagged Announcement or Event would also appear in the block. The umbrella heading reflects the filter; individual cards carry their own `PostType` badge.
- **`/news` feed is unaffected.** Season-tagged posts continue to appear in the global news feed and front-page bulletin alongside untagged posts.
- **Awards key off `ResultWeekDate` outside recap mode.** That field is still set to the last week of the season in recap mode (the cutover predicate is layered on top, not a replacement for week resolution), but the awards query is passed `weekDate: null` when `InRecapMode` is true, matching the `?allAwards=true` codepath.

## Alternatives considered

- **Unconditional replacement once concluded.** Hide weekly results the moment the season ends, regardless of whether recaps exist. Rejected because seasons without recaps would silently lose their results entry point — the page would jump straight to standings — which is worse than the current concluded-season behaviour.
- **Separate `/season/{id}/recaps` sub-page.** Keeps the main season page unchanged and adds a navigation link. Rejected because the goal is for the season URL itself to become the recap-led landing page after the season ends; a sub-page requires visitors to know to click through.
- **Top-of-page banner linking to recaps, weekly results unchanged.** Rejected as too subtle — visitors landing on a concluded season page should see the editorial content as the primary content, not a banner above stale match data.
- **Admin-controlled toggle on the season record.** Rejected as redundant: the data-presence check (`recaps exist`) already gives admins the lever — publishing a recap is the toggle. A separate boolean would be one more thing to forget to flip.
